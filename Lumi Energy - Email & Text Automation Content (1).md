## Notes on Compliance & Deliverability

- **TCPA / texting compliance:** Make sure your original lead form includes clear SMS consent language, and every text after the first includes an opt-out option (e.g., "Reply STOP to unsubscribe"). Add that footer to your actual send templates even though it's not shown above.
- **CAN-SPAM (email):** Include a physical business address and unsubscribe link in your email footer/template.
- **Claims to watch:** Avoid overstating guaranteed savings, rate lock guarantees, or specific dollar figures in mass-send copy unless they're accurate per-lead — keep those personalized on the actual call/quote rather than in the automated sequence.
- **Spacing:** The day intervals above are a starting point — tighten or loosen based on your response data and any state-level texting frequency rules.
