## Implementation notes

### Automation rules

1. **Stop the entire sequence when the lead replies.** Route the conversation to a salesperson.
2. Stop follow-ups when the lead books a consultation or requests a quote.
3. If the lead says they're interested but busy, record their preferred callback time instead of continuing the sequence.
4. If the lead opts out, immediately suppress further marketing texts.
5. Use the customer's first name and the assigned salesperson's real contact information wherever possible.
6. Only send texts to leads with the appropriate SMS marketing consent. Include required opt-out language, such as "Reply STOP to opt out," in the actual SMS templates. Keep the required email unsubscribe mechanism and business address in your email footer.

### What to measure

Track these metrics separately for each touch:

- Positive reply rate
- Callback rate
- Quote-request rate
- Booked consultation rate
- Opt-out rate

**Recommended first tests:** Touches 1–4 and 8. They focus on the missed call, a relatable problem, a visual curiosity hook, year-round use, and a low-effort reply. Keep the messages that generate qualified leads, not merely the most replies.
