Hanging lights every year takes time, it's risky, and it never looks quite right. Then they come down a few weeks later and the whole thing starts over.

================================================================================
WHY HOMEOWNERS CHOOSE IT
================================================================================

Engineered for durability, designed for beauty, and completely customizable from the palm of your hand.

Invisible by Day, Stunning by Night
Your lights sit inside a sleek, UV-protected metal track that we color-match to your home's trim. During the day, the track looks like part of the house. At night, your home comes to life.

Built to Last 20+ Years
Our premium LEDs are rated for over 50,000 hours of use. At typical nightly use, that's more than 20 years of light.

Over 1 Million Color Combinations
You control every light from your phone. Set up red and green for Christmas, orange for Halloween, your team's colors on game day, or a custom scene for any occasion. Schedules and effects run automatically.

True Warm White (RGBWW)
Most permanent lights try to mix a white from red, green, and blue, and the result looks cold or bluish. Our RGBWW lights include dedicated warm white diodes, so you get a soft, inviting glow for everyday accent lighting as well as full color.

Premium Brightness
These are premium, high-output lights and some of the brightest available in the area. Your home will stand out on the street.

Full Workmanship Warranty
We stand behind every installation. If something isn't right with the work, we make it right.
